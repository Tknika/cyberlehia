## **What are your rights when you provide us with your personal data?**

The person who provides their data may exercise the following rights over it:

*Right of access:* The person who provides their data to the Directorate of Technology and Advanced Learning, Deputy Ministry of Vocational Training (Basque Government) may request that data from it in order to confirm that it is processing their personal data.

*Right to rectification:* The person who provides their data may ask the Directorate of Technology and Advanced Learning, Deputy Ministry of Vocational Training (Basque Government) to rectify their personal data if it is incorrect or incomplete.

*Right to erasure:* The person who provides their data may ask the Directorate of Technology and Advanced Learning, Deputy Ministry of Vocational Training (Basque Government) to erase their personal data, among other cases, when it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

*Right to object:* The person who provides their data may object to the Directorate of Technology and Advanced Learning, Deputy Ministry of Vocational Training (Basque Government) processing their personal data. In that case, the Directorate of Technology and Advanced Learning will only keep it in order to make or defend against claims.

*Right to restriction of processing:* The person who provides their data may ask the Directorate of Technology and Advanced Learning, Deputy Ministry of Vocational Training (Basque Government) not to use their personal data temporarily and for specific purposes. In that case, the Directorate of Technology and Advanced Learning will only keep it in order to make or defend against claims.

*Right to data portability:* The person who provides their data may ask the Directorate of Technology and Advanced Learning, Deputy Ministry of Vocational Training (Basque Government) to provide their data in a structured and easily accessible format. Likewise, they may ask it to hand over their data to another controller.

*Right to withdraw consent:* If the personal data was obtained with express consent, the person who provides their data has the right to withdraw that consent at any time.

*Right to lodge a complaint:* The person who provides their data has the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos), in particular if they consider that the exercise of their rights has not been handled properly. The complaint can be made online, through the agency's electronic office ([www.agpd.es](http://www.agpd.es/)), or by writing to the following address: C/ Jorge Juan, 6, 28001 Madrid.

To exercise any of these rights, the person who provides their data may write to the postal address or email address of the Directorate of Technology and Advanced Learning, Deputy Ministry of Vocational Training (Basque Government), enclosing a copy of their national identity document (DNI) so that their identity can be verified.
