We are preparing the **fourth edition** of CyberLehia. As the competition goes on, we'll publish the dates, the results and the information about the grand final on this page.

{{< note >}}
When registration opens, you'll find the link here and on the home page. If you have any questions, write to [cyberlehia@tknika.eus](mailto:cyberlehia@tknika.eus).
{{< /note >}}

## Grand final

The competition ends with a grand final: the finalist teams go head to head in a day that brings together students, schools and companies from the cybersecurity sector. In the 2027 edition, as well as the final of the competition, there will be talks by cybersecurity specialists, technical workshops and company stands.

We'll publish the programme of the 4th edition soon.
