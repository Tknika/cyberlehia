**This website does not use cookies.** We don't use analytics tools, advertising or social media plug-ins, and we don't track your browsing in any way.

## YouTube videos

Videos are shown through YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which does not set cookies. When a video plays, the YouTube player stores some technical data in the browser (*localStorage* and *IndexedDB*) that it needs to work (for example, your subtitle preference). This data does not identify you and is not used for tracking. When the video loads, your IP address reaches Google's servers so that the video can be sent to you.

- **Video lists:** videos don't load until you click on one of them.
- **Home page background video:** it only loads on computers, automatically after the page has loaded; it doesn't load if your device has the reduce motion option, data saver mode or a slow connection turned on. On phones you see a still image.

More information: [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en).

## How to delete this data

You can delete website data (cookies and site data) from your browser settings, or set your browser not to store it.

## Changes

If the website starts using cookies or similar technologies, this page will be updated and, where necessary, your consent will be requested beforehand.
