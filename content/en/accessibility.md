The Directorate of Technology and Advanced Learning (Dirección de Tecnología y Aprendizajes Avanzados), part of the {{< lanbide-heziketa >}}Deputy Ministry of Vocational Training{{< /lanbide-heziketa >}} of the Basque Government, is committed to making its website accessible, in accordance with Spanish Royal Decree 1112/2018, of 7 September, on the accessibility of public sector websites and mobile applications.

This accessibility statement applies to the website [cyberlehia.fpeuskadi.eus](https://cyberlehia.fpeuskadi.eus/en/).

## Compliance status

This website is **partially compliant** with Royal Decree 1112/2018 (standard UNE-EN 301549:2022 and level AA of the WCAG 2.1 guidelines) because of the issues listed below.

## Non-accessible content

The content listed below is not accessible for the following reasons:

### a) Non-compliance with Royal Decree 1112/2018

- The programmes of previous editions' finals (2024, 2025 and 2026 PDFs) are not tagged, so screen readers cannot properly interpret their structure (headings, lists, tables, reading order) [requirements 1.3.1 and 1.3.2]. You can request the same information in an accessible format (see "Comments and contact details").
- Some photos in the previous editions' galleries and in the press room do not have text describing the content of the image [requirement 1.1.1].
- The YouTube videos (talks, testimonials and edition videos) have no transcript or audio description, and their subtitles have not been reviewed [requirements 1.2.2, 1.2.3 and 1.2.5].

### b) Disproportionate burden

Not applicable.

### c) Content not within the scope of the applicable legislation

None.

The registration form is on another website (galdetegiak.tknika.eus) and has not been assessed in this statement.

## Preparation of this accessibility statement

This statement was prepared on **24 November 2026**.

The method used to prepare the statement was a **self-assessment** carried out by the entity itself:

- Automated review of the whole website, in its three languages, with the axe-core tool (WCAG 2.1, levels A and AA), at desktop and mobile screen widths.
- Manual review: keyboard navigation and focus, heading and region structure, colour contrast, text enlargement and reflow at 320 pixels wide, and moving content.

Last review of the statement: **24 November 2026**.

## Comments and contact details

### Communications about accessibility requirements

You can send communications about accessibility requirements (article 10.2.a of Royal Decree 1112/2018), for example to:

- report any possible non-compliance by this website;
- pass on other difficulties in accessing the content;
- make any other query or suggestion for improving the accessibility of the website.

Write to: [cyberlehia@tknika.eus](mailto:cyberlehia@tknika.eus).

### Requests for accessible information and complaints

Under article 10.2.b of Royal Decree 1112/2018, you can also:

- request, in an accessible format, the information in any content that does not meet the accessibility requirements (for example, the text of a PDF programme);
- make a complaint about non-compliance with the accessibility requirements.

You can send these requests to [cyberlehia@tknika.eus](mailto:cyberlehia@tknika.eus) or through the Basque Government's [procedure for complaints and claims regarding the accessibility of websites and apps](https://www.euskadi.eus/quejas-y-reclamaciones-en-materia-de-la-accesibilidad-de-sitios-web-y-apps/web01-tramite/es/) (in Spanish). You will receive a reply within a maximum of **20 working days**.

## Enforcement procedure

Under article 13 of Royal Decree 1112/2018, you can file a claim if:

- your request for accessible information has been refused;
- you disagree with the reply to your request;
- the reply does not meet the requirements of article 12.5;
- twenty working days have passed without a reply.

The claim can be filed through the Basque Government's [procedure for complaints and claims regarding the accessibility of websites and apps](https://www.euskadi.eus/quejas-y-reclamaciones-en-materia-de-la-accesibilidad-de-sitios-web-y-apps/web01-tramite/es/) (in Spanish). Claims will be received and handled by the Directorate of Citizen Services and Administrative Innovation and Improvement (Dirección de Atención a la Ciudadanía e Innovación y Mejora de la Administración).

## Optional content

This website includes the following accessibility aids:

- A "Skip to content" link at the start of every page, for keyboard users.
- All pages can be used with a keyboard and the focus is always visible. The main menu's submenus close with the Esc key.
- The layout adapts to any screen size and text can be enlarged to 200% without losing content.
- The home page background video has no sound and can be paused with a button. If the device has the reduce motion option turned on, the video is not loaded and animations are turned off; it is not loaded in data saver mode or on slow connections either.
- The site is in Basque, Spanish and English, and each page states its language.
- Links that open another website in a new window announce this to screen readers.
